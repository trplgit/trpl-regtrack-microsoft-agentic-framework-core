# Free Monthly Insights - Location

About the reader's sites. It answers: is my overdue work spread across my sites, or does it
sit at a few, and which ones?

## What this email states, in this order

Every item below is a paragraph: its figures, each with its base, then ONE meaning sentence
from the shared rules (section 4a). Figures without their meaning sentence are not enough.

1. **In {{PREV_MONTH}}** - four short sentences, then the meaning sentence: "In
   {{PREV_MONTH}}, N compliances from that month are still open today. They sit at N of your
   M locations. N locations left a higher share of their {{PREV_MONTH}} work open than the
   other locations. They include your {{EG_1}} site, with N of its M still open, and your
   {{EG_2}} site, with all N still open."
2. **In total, from all months** - the overdue total, how many are overdue for more than 90
   days, how many carry personal liability. Then the meaning sentence. Nothing else in this
   paragraph.
2a. **The one strongest pattern of locations** - its own short paragraph: EITHER the
   locations where every open compliance rests with one person ("At N of your M locations,
   every open compliance rests with one person.") OR the 3-location share ("Your 3 locations
   with the most overdue compliances hold P% of all M overdue compliances.") - the one-person
   sites whenever that count is above 0 - then the meaning sentence. The counts of locations
   with liability work or 90-day work are NOT stated; they stay in RegInsights Ultimate.
3. **The named sites** - {{NAME_1}} and {{NAME_2}} in full, one paragraph; {{NAME_3}} and {{NAME_4}} counted with the others and pointed at RegInsights Ultimate (shared rules section 6):
   - liability share: "At your {{NAME_1}} site, N of its M overdue compliances carry
     personal liability, or P%, compared with P% across your organisation." Then the
     residual in its own sentence.
   - concentration: "Your {{NAME_2}} site holds N of the M overdue compliances
     across your organisation, or P%." Then the residual in its own sentence.
   - one-person site: "At your {{NAME_3}} site, all N open compliances rest with one person."
4. **Before the end of {{CURR_MONTH}}** - what still falls due, and the part with personal
   liability.

## Units

Sites and compliances never share a clause. A site with no compliances configured cannot be
assessed; say that nothing is configured there, never that it is performing well.

**Length:** about 300 words after `Good morning,`, 5 paragraphs.
